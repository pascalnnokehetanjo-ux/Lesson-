# LESSONCHIP — From Curriculum to Classroom

## Overview
LESSONCHIP is a multilingual, mobile-first
educational planning platform designed to help
Cameroon nursery and primary school teachers
turn official curriculum requirements into
structured, classroom-ready lesson plans.

## Vision
From Curriculum to Classroom.

## Intended Users
- Nursery and primary school teachers
- Headteachers and school administrators
- Educational inspectors

## Planned Features
- English and French curriculum support
- Class, subject, and term selection
- Schemes of work
- Monthly Integrated Learning Themes (ILTs)
  and projects
- Weekly lesson plans
- Daily lesson plans with objectives, activities,
  teaching materials, assessment, and
  curriculum-aligned content notes
- Downloadable and printable teaching documents
- Mobile-first, low-bandwidth design

## Curriculum Integrity
Generated lesson plans should be grounded in
the relevant official curriculum for the selected
subsystem, class, subject, term, and topic.
Curriculum data must be added and verified
before it is treated as an authoritative source.

## Development Status

be implemented.
